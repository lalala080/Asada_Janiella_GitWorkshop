Genshin Impact

About the Game

Genshin Impact is an open-world action role-playing game. Players can explore the world of Teyvat, discover new places, and meet different characters.

Featured Characters

Lumine

The Traveler who explores Teyvat.

Raiden Shogun

The Electro Archon of Inazuma.

Nahida

The Dendro Archon of Sumeru.

Game Features

  • Open-world exploration
  • Elemental combat
  • Multiple playable characters
  • Beautiful environments
